How to Use

Follow these simple steps for the best results with our herbal powders.

Take 2–3 tablespoons of powder in a bowl and mix with lukewarm water or curd until it forms a smooth paste.

Apply the paste evenly on your scalp and along the length of your hair. Massage gently for better absorption.

Leave it for 20–30 minutes. Rinse thoroughly with normal water. For best results, use 2–3 times a week.
